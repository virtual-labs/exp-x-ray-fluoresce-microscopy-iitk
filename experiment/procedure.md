<b> Methodology:</b><br>
<b> Determination of chemical composition:</b>
The XRF spectroscopy component’s, analysing crystals receive the radiation from collimators (used to control the beam divergence, received from sample). It disperses the x-rays according to Bragg’s law, where the number of different crystal (LiF, Ge, pentaerythritol, thallium acid phthalate) and crystal fluorescence (for background noise) are used to cover a wide range of wavelengths. Then the detector receive the diffracted beam from analysing crystal which convert the radiation into measurable voltage pulses. Further, these pulses converted to height distribution plot (PHD).<br>
